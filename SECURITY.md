Use harmonious colors and make the game engaging. Allow players to customize their characters with an anime style, providing each character with 100 outfits. Include houses with stairs and animals. Add American music sung by a girl, allowing players to change the song or mute the sound. Design a map for each level, give the characters names, and make the game visually interesting and brightly lit.
